# Names and logos

"Pat", "Pat, the Phone Agent Tool" and "Oasis Team", and any Pat logo or mascot picture (together, the "Marks"), identify the
official software from Oasis Team. The Apache License 2.0 that covers the code gives no right to use them (its Section 6).
This policy says what you may do. It does not change the Apache License, and it gives no right beyond what it says.

## You may

- Say truthfully where something came from, such as "based on Pat" or "works with Pat", and keep the notices the license requires.
- Use the Marks to refer to the official software in articles, reviews and tutorials.

## If you modify or fork Pat, you must

- Not use the Marks as the name of your version, or in its logo, app name or domain name.
- State clearly that your version is modified, is not official, and is not made, approved or supported by Oasis Team, and link
  to the official Pat repository.
- Not suggest in any way that Oasis Team endorses you or your version.

## Never allowed

- Using the Marks on a version that removes, weakens or skips Pat's statement at the start of a call that the caller is an AI,
  or that tells the AI to deny being an AI. Such a version is not Pat, and Oasis Team does not stand behind any call made with it.
- Using the Marks to mislead anyone about who is calling.
